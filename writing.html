The Stoic Mom

I started The Stoic Mom in October 2016 and have written it ever since, first at thestoicmom.com and on Substack since 2022, where I've published more than fifty posts. Much of that writing pushes back on what's been made of this philosophy online: the “broicism” of cold self-reliance, and the $toicism that sells ancient virtue as a productivity hack.
