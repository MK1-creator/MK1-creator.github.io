How I work

I help clients set meaningful goals and find real ways to work toward them. Many are focused on growing their personal leadership.

Co-Active coaching starts from the premise that you are naturally creative, resourceful, and whole — my role isn't to hand you answers but to help you find your own. Stoic practice adds a useful discipline on top of that: distinguishing what's genuinely in your control from what isn't, and spending your energy accordingly.
