What I have led

Three programs that show how AI and technology communications becomes an organizational capability: how a function gets built, how technical work reaches executive decisions, and how it travels to products and the public.

Case study 01

Building Adobe Research communications from the ground up

The challenge
Adobe Research had no communications practice. Its work in AI, machine learning, computer vision, and graphics had no consistent route to executives, product teams, or the public.
What I led
I joined in 2017 as the organization's founding science writer and built the function from there, becoming Director of Research and Innovation Communications. I wrote and published its first stories on AI, machine learning, computer vision, and graphics, then developed the practice around them.
People and functions involved
Researchers and research leadership, plus partners across marketing, social, talent marketing, PR, legal, design, and product.
The system created
A news site and technical blog, social channels, a video series, and an editorial operating rhythm that made publication repeatable rather than occasional.
Supporting metrics
37% year-over-year growth in original research news output.

Case study 02

Turning emerging AI into executive alignment

The result
